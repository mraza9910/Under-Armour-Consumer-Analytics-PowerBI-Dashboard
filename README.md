## Project Overview

This project analyzes consumer survey data to understand Under Armour's position within the athletic apparel and footwear market.

The analysis examines consumer demographics, competitive brand positioning, purchase consideration, style and pricing preferences, shopping behavior, sports participation, and potential go-to-market opportunities.

The dashboard was designed to transform respondent-level survey data into an interactive analytical tool for exploring consumer behavior and identifying patterns associated with Under Armour purchase consideration.

## Dashboard Sections

The Power BI report contains six analytical views:

1. **Demographics Overview**  
   Examines the composition of the respondent sample across age, gender, race, geography, and survey completion characteristics.

2. **Brand Awareness & Perception**  
   Compares brand familiarity, purchase consideration, recent footwear purchases, and reported reasons for brand avoidance.

3. **Purchase Drivers, Style Attitudes & Pricing**  
   Analyzes footwear budgets, product purchase drivers, style attitudes, and their relationship with Under Armour purchase consideration.

4. **Shopping Behaviors & Preferences**  
   Examines retailer penetration, annual purchase frequency, and preferences for online versus in-store shopping.

5. **Sports Engagement & Brand Influence**  
   Explores respondent sports participation, fitness intensity, brand loyalty, and Under Armour purchase consideration.

6. **Customer Engagement & Go-To-Market Opportunities**  
   Evaluates digital engagement channels, notification preferences, dedicated app preference, preferred music platforms, and payment method.
   
## Business Recommendations and Insights

- **Price and Design Preferences**
   - 48% of respondents cite high prices as a top reason for brand avoidance. The $100–$124 footwear price range is the most preferred (22.9%).
   - Respondents identify footwear variety and minimalist designs across products as key purchase drivers, followed by peer influence and product quality.
- **Nike is a Strong Competitor**
   - 68% of respondents indicate familiarity with Nike, compared with 25% for Under Armour. Brand familiarity is defined as having heard of the brand or having personally owned, or knowing a peer who owns, 1–5 pairs of shoes from the brand.
   - Nike has a Brand Consideration Score of 4.1, compared with 3.2 for Under Armour. Brand consideration is measured on a 1–5 scale, where 1 = Very Unlikely and 5 = Very Likely to consider purchasing.
   - 35.3% of respondents reported Nike as their most recently purchased footwear brand, compared with 2.9% for Under Armour.
- **Shopping Patterns**
   - 79% and 73% of respondents report shopping at Amazon and Target, respectively. This highlights potential retail partnership opportunities to expand Under Armour's customer reach and increase sales.
   - 42.86% of respondents prefer shopping in-store for footwear, compared with 28.79% for apparel. Under Armour could benefit from strengthening its physical retail presence to capture footwear sales.
- **GTM**
   - 49.61% of respondents listen to music on Spotify. Under Armour should consider targeted advertising campaigns on Spotify, followed by YouTube (20.4% of the respondents listen to music on YT), to reach its target customer base.
   - 80.99% of respondents express interest in a dedicated Under Armour shopping app. Under Armor could offer app-exclusive discounts to encourage adoption and increase customer engagement.
   - 13.21% and 12.41% of respondents use Apple/Google Pay and Cash App, respectively. This highlights opportunities to integrate digital payment options and improve the customer checkout experience.
