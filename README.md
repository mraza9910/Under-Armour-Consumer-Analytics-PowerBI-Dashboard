## Project Overview

This project analyzes consumer survey data to better understand Under Armour's position within the athletic apparel and footwear market.

The analysis examines consumer demographics, competitive brand positioning, purchase consideration, style and pricing preferences, shopping behavior, sports participation, and potential go-to-market opportunities.

The dashboard was designed to transform respondent-level survey data into an interactive analytical tool for exploring consumer behavior and identifying patterns associated with Under Armour purchase consideration.

## Dashboard Sections

The Power BI report contains six analytical views:

1. **Demographics Overview**  
   Examines the composition of the respondent sample across age, gender, race, geography, and survey completion characteristics.

2. **Brand Awareness & Perception**  
   Compares brand familiarity, purchase consideration, recent footwear purchases, and reported reasons for brand avoidance.

3. **Purchase Drivers, Style Attitudes & Pricing**  
   Analyzes footwear budgets, product purchase drivers, style attitudes, and their relationship with Under Armour purchase consideration.

4. **Shopping Behaviors & Preferences**  
   Examines retailer penetration, annual purchase frequency, and preferences for online versus in-store shopping.

5. **Sports Engagement & Brand Influence**  
   Explores sports participation, fitness intensity, brand loyalty, and Under Armour purchase consideration.

6. **Customer Engagement & Go-To-Market Opportunities**  
   Evaluates digital engagement channels, notification preferences, app usage, music platforms, and payment behavior.

## Data

The analysis is based on survey responses from **1,176 respondents**.

### Data Confidentiality

The underlying respondent-level dataset used for this project is confidential and cannot be publicly distributed. Therefore, the source dataset is not included in this repository.

The interactive dashboard is provided to demonstrate the analytical methodology, data modeling, visualization design, and business insights developed during the project.
